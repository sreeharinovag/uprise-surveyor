06 / About

Uprise Surveyor

A land surveying practice based in Vazhithala, Thodupuzha, Idukki, Kerala, built around RTK satellite positioning and digital deliverables: boundary determination, contour and topographic survey, construction layout and mapping.

The approach is simple: measure carefully, check the measurement, and hand over data that can be opened, understood and used immediately.

High precision and fast service are not a trade-off here. Modern instruments remove the slow parts of the job, and the time saved goes back into verification.
